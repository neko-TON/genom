01

WHAT HAPPENS
EVERY 24 HOURS

  1. 00

    Launched on Pons V2, Robinhood Chain

    $NSTRO starts on the Pons bonding curve and graduates into a Uniswap v4 pool with permanently locked liquidity. Trading fees are charged by the launchpad in ETH and routed straight into the contracts below — nothing is manual.

    pons v2
  2. 01

    Every trade pays 3%

    80% buys the holders' basket, 13% funds the agent's operations, 7% goes to the protocol. Creator fees add 1% in USDG. Nobody has to do anything — the flow never stops.

    80 / 13 / 7
  3. 02

    Shares accrue by balance × time

    Not by snapshot. A bot entering one block before the close earns nothing. Claim whenever you want — there is no distribution schedule to snipe.

    bal × t
  4. 03

    The decision is sealed before the trade

    The agent publishes keccak256(epoch, weights, thesis, nonce) before anything moves. Reasoning goes on record first, outcome comes second — never the other way around.

    keccak256
  5. 04

    The contract checks the vector

    Whitelist only. Weight ≤ 35%. Position ≥ 5%. Turnover ≤ 20% NAV. One rebalance per epoch. Cash 0–100%. Any failure cancels the epoch and the basket stays put.

    8 checks
  6. 05

    Execution is one batch

    Swaps go through Uniswap V4 pools together. Slippage over the limit reverts everything — no partial fills, no surprises.

    1 batch
  7. 06

    The thesis is revealed and re-hashed

    Next epoch, the text is opened and verified against the sealed hash. A mismatch marks the epoch failed — publicly, forever. The track record cannot be rewritten.

    N + 1

THE DOWNSIDE IS ENGINEERED,
NOT PROMISED

A

The agent breaks

Worst case: whitelisted stocks in bad proportions, at most a fifth of NAV turned over per day. Withdrawing assets is not a function its key has.

B

Drawdown hits −25% in 7 epochs

The breaker freezes the agent and parks the basket in USDG. Only a holder vote with a 3-day timelock switches it back on.

C

Holders stop trusting it

Limits, whitelist, the agent itself — all replaceable by vote. Three days of timelock give dissenters time to exit first.
